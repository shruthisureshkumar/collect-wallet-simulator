Quick guide

Step 1 is the internal question this simulator helps answer. Steps 2–5 mirror what a merchant actually does.

  1. 1 A merchant never sees this choice — it's what we're deciding: whether Standard Prepaid or Credit Inclusive is the better model for Collect. Pick one at the top to simulate under it; the Plans table shows what each tier costs and charges.
  2. 2 They also choose a settlement timeline — T+1 (next working day) or T0 (instant, at a slightly higher rate). Set it here; it locks in, just like a live plan.
  3. 3 When a merchant pays a plan's upfront cost, it lands in their wallet as credits. Add a top-up with a plan and date to simulate that payment.
  4. 4 Every day, a merchant has customer collections due for presentation against their wallet. Add one with an amount and due date to simulate that.
  5. 5 From here it plays out as it would for a real merchant — top-up reminders before a shortfall, each collection settling gross or net, and rates shifting on plan changes — all in the ledger below.
